Steps for Part A
  1. Slide all three riders to zero. With the pan empty, turn the zero knob until the pointer rests on the orange zero line.
  2. Place your object on the pan.
  3. Move the 100 g rider one notch at a time until the pointer drops below zero. Move it back one notch.
  4. Do the same with the 10 g rider. Then slide the 1 g rider slowly until the pointer sits exactly on zero.
  5. Add the three rider readings. Record the mass in grams (g).

Steps for Part B
  1. Pour in enough water to cover the object. Read the bottom of the meniscus at eye level. Record the Initial Water Volume.
  2. Tilt the cylinder and slide the object in. Do not drop it.
  3. Read the new level at the bottom of the meniscus. Record the Final Water Volume.
  4. Final Water Volume − Initial Water Volume = Volume of the Object.
