Teacher

Build a set

Once per Quizlet set. About 15–20 minutes the first time, less once your checked words start being reused.

  1. Quizlet website

    Copy the export

    Open your set, choose ⋯ → Export, keep the default settings, and press Copy text.

    Only sets you created can be exported. For a set you copied from someone else, make your own version first.

  2. Studio · Import

    Paste and read the cards

    Press New set, paste into Export text, and press Read cards. Add a title (students see it) and the unit. Fix any row marked yellow.

    A .csv file or a saved deck file works too, through Open a file….

  3. Studio · Translate

    Let Claude translate

    Tick the languages your students need, keep plain-English definitions on, and press Translate all. Allow Claude the first time it asks.

    About 1–3 minutes for 20 cards. If it won't run, use Translate in a Claude chat on the same step.

  4. Studio · Review

    Check each language

    Pick a language, fix anything that's wrong, and tick Check on each card. Read the yellow notes Claude leaves about tricky words.

    Press Copy … list for EL staff to send a language to a colleague, especially Kreyòl. Checked words are reused in future sets.

  5. Studio · Pictures

    Fix the pictures

    Press Change on any card whose icon doesn't fit, or Upload your own image. Quizlet images don't come across.

  6. Studio · Test & export

    Set the test and preview

    Choose the number of questions and question types. Look over the checklist, then press Preview student page and try it as a student.

  7. Google Sites

    Put it on your site

    Press Copy page HTML. In Sites choose Insert → Embed → Embed code, paste, then Next → Insert. Stretch the box full width and about 680 px tall. Publish.

    Also press Download deck file to keep a backup copy you can edit later.

  8. Google Classroom

    Post the assignment

    Share the Sites link and ask students to paste their Flashcard Bridge record as their answer, or attach the PDF.

Student

Study a set

In class or at home on a Chromebook or phone. Every button shows English first with the student's language underneath.

  1. Google Classroom

    Open the link

    The Settings panel opens by itself the first time.

  2. Settings

    Choose name, language and support

    Type your name and class hour. Pick English only, Español, Français, Kreyòl or العربية, then a support level (see the table below). The page remembers these on that computer.

  3. Flashcards

    Meet the words

    Tap the card or press Space to flip. Then choose Got it or Still learning. The 🔊 button reads English, then your language.

    At the end, press Study my Still learning cards to go again with just those.

  4. Learn

    Practice with choices

    Pick an answer, then press Check. A wrong answer shows which card you picked and comes back later. Get a card right 2 times in a row to master it.

  5. Match

    Play Match

    Tap a card, then tap its partner. Newcomers can match English ↔ my language. Try to beat your best time.

  6. Test

    Take the practice test

    Answer every question, write the short answer in English using the sentence starters, and press Finish test. Right answers show at the end.

    Press Study the cards I missed to go straight back to Flashcards.

  7. Google Classroom

    Turn it in

    Press Copy record and paste it into the Classroom assignment. Or press Download PDF and attach the file.

    If copying doesn't work, the record is highlighted. Press Ctrl + C.

When things change

Three situations you'll run into after the first set, and what to do.

A translation is wrong

Fix and re-export

The fix is saved for next time too.

My sets→Open→Review→Copy page HTML→Edit embed in Sites
You add terms in Quizlet

Add them in the Studio

Open the set, press + Add a card on Import, then Translate the cards that still need it. Nothing else is redone.

Import→Translate→Review new cards→Export
A student scores low

Send them back to study

The record lists every missed term. Students can press Study the cards I missed and retake a new test.

Record→Flashcards→Learn→New test

Which support level?

Students choose in Settings and can change it anytime. A starting point you can suggest:

LevelGood starting point forWhat the student sees
Full supportNewcomers, WIDA 1–2Their language always on the card, the plain-English definition first, 3 answer choices, and Match set to English ↔ my language
Some supportWIDA 3–4English on the card, with a Show my language button when they need it, and 4 answer choices
No supportGen-ed students, WIDA 5–6A clean English-only page that works like Quizlet

Good to know

  • 1Progress stays on the device.Each student's progress is saved in that Chromebook's browser. The turn-in record is what counts.
  • 2Kreyòl read-aloud.Most Chromebooks have no Kreyòl voice, so the speaker reads English only and says so. Arabic voices are sometimes missing too.
  • 3Keep the Studio private.Anyone you share it with could see your saved sets. Share the Google Site with students, never the Studio.
  • 4“Machine translated” note.Students see it for any language you haven't fully checked. It goes away once every card in that language is checked.
  • 5Nothing to log in to.Students need no accounts, and no student data leaves their device except the record they choose to paste.
  • 6Translation uses your Claude account.Checked words are reused, so each new set costs less than the one before.
